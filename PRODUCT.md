# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ChenDoc is for a single owner or a very small private publishing workflow. The primary user signs in to write, manage, review, publish, and recover personal documents from a lightweight local-first admin surface.

## Product Purpose

ChenDoc provides a focused document management and knowledge publishing system with one dependable local entry point. Success means the owner can get into the workspace quickly, trust the auth and publishing flow, and avoid heavyweight enterprise or groupware patterns.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, personal. The interface should feel clean and trustworthy, with the anime wallpaper and logo acting as ChenDoc's memory points rather than taking over the task.

## Anti-references

Do not turn ChenDoc into enterprise OA, team collaboration software, or a marketing landing page. Avoid generic purple AI dashboards, heavy glass cards, busy illustration systems, beige editorial themes, and security flows that interrupt low-risk local login by default.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the working path short: login, write, publish, and recover should stay direct.
2. Let documents carry the interface: use white surfaces, clear typography, and restrained blue actions.
3. Preserve ChenDoc identity: keep the existing anime wallpaper and logo as brand assets.
4. Treat security as adaptive: show friction only when the request, device, or credential behavior indicates risk.
5. Stay lightweight: prefer existing Vue, local fonts, lucide icons, and project tokens over new UI frameworks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, visible focus states, 44px touch targets on mobile, and reduced-motion-safe transitions. Chinese UI copy should stay concise and task-oriented.
